What is gaggle.fun?


A place to play voice-chat integrated games and have adventures with your friends.

General

What do I need?


Sign up, download the game you want to play, and get started! Our games are designed to work with most laptops and mobile devices, headphones recommended. Wits are entirely optional.

General

Can I play on my phone or tablet?


Check on the specific game title you wish to play. We're desktop/laptop focused right now but we will be releasing to more platforms later. For Goose Goose Duck, you can play with iOS and Android!

General

I'm on a Mac, will this work for me?


It should, but sometimes you'll need to give our applications special permissions to let you share your microphone.

General

Do I need to download separate voice chat software?


Nope! It's all integrated into the game you download.

General

How can I delete my Gaggle account?


If you have a problem or concern, we can help! If you would like to permanently delete your account, please visit "My Account", click "Settings", and then click "Delete account". Just be sure you have the right account, once you delete your account it’s gone forever!

My Account

I’ve made a purchase, but I don’t see it in my account!


When you make a purchase through Steam, Steam will first authenticate the transaction and place your funds in your Steam wallet. Once the funds are there, try to purchase the pack or gold coin bundle again. Restart the game, and your purchased items should be on your account.

Do I have to play with strangers, or can I play with friends?


Up to you! If you want to play with your friends, just pick the map that you want to play, host a game, and share the room code with your friends. Or, you can play in public matches with strangers. Find a game, see which game mode and map that you want, and have fun!

How do I report someone/remove someone from the game?


You can block or mute the relevant player in-game and use the in-game censoring filter. You can report the offending player as well, and these reports will be attached to their account, and may result in bans. If you are the lobby host, you can kick out the offending players, and or you can ask the host to kick them. Players who are kicked will not be able to rejoin the game unless allowed back in by the host. If the player in question is the host, and a sufficient number of players block their mics, they will be kicked from the game, and once again that will be reflected on their account.

We have a great community on Discord, where there are players and streamers and developers available. We highly recommend joining our Duckscord server, if you ever have any questions or need any technical help.
